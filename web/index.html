Welcome to Piano Typer

  • 1 key
    One key = one letter. Every letter key is labelled on the keyboard below.
  • chord
    Press a word's letters together to type the whole word. Order doesn't matter — “stop”, “pots” and “spot” are the same chord, and the AI picks the right one from context.
  • laptop
    Laid out like a laptop keyboard folded flat: ⌫ backspace, ␣ space (middle C), ×2 double letter and ✓ complete sit in the middle under your thumbs. Left-hand letters are left of them, right-hand letters right, with the most used letters closest to the middle. Shift, undo, delete word and S1–S3 are on the far left; punctuation and enter on the right, then digits.
  • glow
    Glowing keys are likely next letters. Dotted outlines show the chord for the top suggested word.
  • laptop
    No piano plugged in? Type on your laptop or click the keys. Hold several laptop keys together to play a chord; Shift+click builds a chord with the mouse. Tab accepts the first suggestion.
